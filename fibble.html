FIBBLE
Lie. Vote. Win.

Everyone writes a convincing lie. Spot the real answer — or fool the whole room.

Built-in question bank plus your own questions. Works fully offline — everything is stored on this device.

How to play

A trivia game where the lies matter as much as the truth.

1

Get a question

Each round shows a strange-but-true fact with one key word missing. Everyone sees the same blank.

2

Write a lie

Pass the device around. Each player secretly types a fake answer that sounds convincing. You can't submit the real answer — and you can't copy someone else's lie.

3

Vote for the truth

All the lies are shuffled together with the real answer. Pass the device again and each player picks the one they think is true. You can't vote for your own lie.

4

Score points

+500 for finding the truth, and +500 for every player your lie fools. The final round can be worth double.

5

Win

After the last round, the highest score wins. Bragging rights included.

Pass & play

How it connects

Devices talk through a free public MQTT relay over the internet — there is no server to set up. Rooms are separated by a random code. The relay can technically see game traffic, so don't play with real secrets.
